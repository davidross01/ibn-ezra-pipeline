# House rules — Hebrew, Misprints and variants

Part of commentary-house-rules (the core is the skill itself). Moved here word for word on 27 Oct 2026; change a rule here and in the core line that points to it.

## Hebrew
- Use only vowelled source PDFs. Never change a Hebrew letter of the source, and never retype Hebrew from memory: substitute inside existing strings, and match passages by consonants (strip vowels and cantillation).
- Restoring letters lost in the PDF text layer is allowed only when a second witness (e.g. Sefaria) supplies the same letter.
- When showing Hebrew to David, show the Hebrew itself, pointed, with no phonetic transliteration.
- **The letters of the Hebrew commentary are Sefaria's, letter for letter** (from Noach on; the note on the vowels promises it). When pointing, never shorten or lengthen a word: if Ibn Ezra spells a word fully (תואר, גופר, ביאר), keep his ו/י and move the vowel onto it (תּוֹאַר, גוֹפֶר, בֵּיאֵר); never change a letter to the Bible's spelling or to a "better" word (Noach draft 1 at first had silently made דעת into דעה, ומת into וימת, החת into החתי). Only a misprint correction in [square brackets] may add letters. Check by program before every draft: noach/work/consonant_check.py strips vowels, markup and [corrections] and compares every word with Sefaria (it must print "differences: 0"); noach/work/fix_letters.py restores Sefaria's ו/י automatically where only a vowel letter differs and lists the rest for hand fixing.
- **Ibn Ezra's own spelling in the headings** (David, Noach Review List 2 row A1): where his lemma is spelled differently from the verse (הַהִיא for הַהִוא, Noach 10:11–12), the English-side heading of the comment and of the note uses his spelling, as his Hebrew commentary does; the verse band keeps the verse's spelling; no Text line.
- **Written and read forms (ketiv/qere) in the verse band** (David, row B5 of Noach Review List 1, Oct 2026, for the whole book): print the written form unpointed and without brackets, then the reading pointed in square brackets: הוצא [הַיְצֵא] (8:17). Sefaria's MAM marks these with `mam-kq` spans; check every parasha for them (Bereshit has none; Noach only 8:17). The note gives the written form and says which one Ibn Ezra comments on.
- **Scripture quotations and lemmas take the Bible's vowels** (Miqra according to the Masorah, without cantillation or meteg, keeping its in-context dagesh, its qamatz qatan and its pausal forms, e.g. וַיָּמׇת, קׇדָשִׁים, פַּלְגֵי מָיִם), carried over to Ibn Ezra's spelling. **Words Sefaria prints already pointed inside Ibn Ezra keep Sefaria's marks in the Hebrew commentary**, even lemmas and quotations (וְנָבֵלה, וְנָסֵבָה at Noach 11:7; וַתַּקח, וַתִּשְלְכִי at Lech Lecha 12:15, David's row B2), with no Text line. Abbreviations, ד' (in the Hebrew commentary), letter names (בי"ת, הנון, כיו"ד) and book names and numbers in references stay unpointed. Check with noach/work/vowel_check.py (lemmas against the verse and its neighbours, quotations against their references) and read every hit: many are look-alike words, not errors.
- The verse band follows Miqra according to the Masorah even where common printed Chumashim spell a word differently (row B6, Noach: 7:11 מַעְיְנֹת, 9:29 וַיִּהְיוּ).
- The vowels of Ibn Ezra's own words were added by Claude and are not checked against a vowelled edition (David chose Keep on a sample check, row B1 of Review List 3). The preface and the note on the vowels say so; keep that statement true.

## Misprints and variants
- A misprinted word is shown in the Hebrew as (printed) [correction], the correction pointed. The English follows the correction unless David decides otherwise. Every correction gets a short {i}Text.{/i} line in the note on the same verse. Where David chooses to leave a misprint uncorrected (Lech Lecha 12:11 יהי', row A1), the Hebrew shows only the printed text and there is no Text line.
- **A wrong verse reference: the correction goes inside the source's round brackets** (David, Oct 2026): {s}(תהלים קמח ה [קמח ח]){/s}, not (תהלים קמח ה) [קמח ח]. The printed reference stays; the right one follows it in square brackets, all within the one pair of round brackets.
- Edition brackets and variant readings (צ"ל, נ"א, manuscripts) are a translation choice for David, never a silent fix. An emendation that is more than a one-letter slip (Noach 10:21 אבי הם → אָבִיו חָם) is marked as such in its review row.
