# House rules — Page look (Seforno-volume template)

Part of commentary-house-rules (the core is the skill itself). Moved here word for word on 27 Oct 2026; change a rule here and in the core line that points to it.

## Page look (Seforno-volume template)
- Verse band on top in the Hebrew Chumash look (pointed, bold, cantillation), English beside it. **The Chumash Hebrew is a little smaller than at first** (David, Oct 2026): 19.6 px on 28 px lines (was 21.2/30). **The English and the Hebrew start on the same line** (David, Oct 2026): the band aligns its two columns on their first baseline (book.css `.band { align-items: first baseline; }`); before, the English sat 8 px higher. No rishon labels and no boxed aliyah marks.
- **Section marks** (David, Oct 2026). After a verse that ends a section of the Torah scroll, the verse band prints the letter: פ for פָּרָשָׁה פְּתוּחָה (an "open portion": the text before it ends, and the new section begins on a fresh line) and ס for פָּרָשָׁה סְתוּמָה (a "closed portion": a gap in the middle of a line). The mark comes from the verse's `brk` field and is set small and bold after the verse (`.band .brk`); both columns start a new paragraph there. The parasha ends with פ פ פ. The preface ("How to read a page") explains both letters.
- **A new chapter starts a new paragraph in the verse band** (David, row E7, Oct 2026), in both the Hebrew and the English, as well as after a פ or ס (layout.js bandHTML). In every parasha, Bereshit included since 25 Oct 2026 (Noach Review List 2 row B1).
- Hebrew commentary (RTL), divider, English commentary, rule, two-column notes, and a foot-of-page strip glossing the grammar terms on that page.
- **Notes stay with their verse's page wherever they fit** (David, row E5, Oct 2026): layout.js NB = 100 (was 440), so notes run on to the next page only when they don't fit; a few pages then end with some white space, which is accepted. In every parasha, Bereshit included (Noach Review List 2 row B1).
- **No chapter numbers in the notes** (David, row E6, Oct 2026): the notes run on without chapter headings (render.py skips the chapter markers for the notes stream; the English commentary keeps them). In every parasha, Bereshit included (Noach Review List 2 row B1).
- **Comments go on the page of their verse.** (David, Oct 2026: "it makes sense that it should be there as the verse is on that page".) The page-fitter (scripts/layout.js) gives each page its verses and then ALL the Hebrew and English comments on those verses, and fills the rest with notes; notes may run on to the next page. Only when one verse's comments are longer than a page (1:1, 1:26 in Bereshit) do they continue overleaf, Hebrew and English advancing together. If a page would otherwise be left more than about 15% empty (STRETCH = 0.15), the next verse starts at the foot of the page with the beginning of its Hebrew and English comments, continuing overleaf. Don't lower this to fill a few more pages: at 0.09 it split six more verses' comments across pages (David on short pages, row C3: "you don't need to go crazy"). A parasha never ends on a page of leftover notes. Nothing may run into a divider: all fonts are loaded before measuring, and the final check in layout.js reports any overlap ("WARN overlap" / "WARN past bottom") — a draft with such a warning is not sent. After each build, check which page each verse's comments and notes land on and the empty space per page, and look at the pages as images.
- **Foot-of-page definitions must be very simple.** (David's rule, Oct 2026.) Each term in the strip gets a plain, clear definition that anyone can understand, with a short everyday example where it helps (e.g. construct — a word joined to the next one by "of": "house of the king"). No technical words inside a definition. Vowels show their sign on an alef (אָ), the dagesh in ב (בּ). The strip takes up to three lines (four, slightly smaller, on a crowded page) so that every term used on the page is listed; the build warns "strip dropped" if one is left out. If that happens, shorten a definition rather than drop the term (draft 8: "heavy conjugation" became just "Ibn Ezra's name for the pi'el" so "suffix" fits on page 8). Bereshit's list (data/foot_terms.json) is the starting point for the next parasha.
- Lemmas bold. For emphasis in a grammar example, enlarge the English word that carries the point, not the Hebrew.
- Front: title, contents, preface, how this edition was made, two-page key to terms (◆ where Ibn Ezra differs from modern grammar), then the note on the vowels (10 pages, i–x; David, row E3, Oct 2026: at the beginning of the book only, never at the end of any parasha; it lives in bereshit/data/front.json 'before'). **Each key-to-terms entry ends with "First:" and its first place in each parasha** (Front Matter RL1 row C5, applied 25 Oct 2026; small grey `.keytab .kf`): when a parasha becomes final, run review_round/key_places.py / apply_key.py logic for it (term in the Hebrew commentary + house English in the English commentary of the same verse), read every candidate (review_round/key_verify.py), and add its first place to each entry. Each parasha ends with פ פ פ.
- **A glossary at the back of the book** (David, 9 Nov 2026: “add a section which will be at the back of the book which has all of the definitions of words which a reader might be confused on which are currently at the very bottom of the page … it should be in alphabetical order”; his choices: the back of the full book only, keep the foot strip too, a separate plain glossary with the key to terms left as it is). Every term of the foot strip in the final parashot (`<folder>/data/foot_terms.json`), once each, A–Z, with the same plain definition as in the strip, in a two-column table (term, definition). It is built by `bereshit/scripts/render.py data/model_final5.json build/back.html backonly`, numbered on from the last final page (183–184 now), running head GLOSSARY, and written to `back/Ibn Ezra on the Torah - Glossary.pdf` by scripts/publish_pdfs.py every time the book is joined (front matter, final parashot, glossary). The Contents lists it after the parashot, and the preface's “Terms at the foot of the page” says it is there. A term added to a parasha's foot_terms.json reaches the glossary when that parasha is final; never edit the glossary by hand.
- **Front and back matter end above the foot** (9 Nov 2026): layout.js warns “past bottom … (front/back matter)” when a static page's text runs below 980 px (these pages have no strip). The check found the key to terms page vii running off the page (nif’al, hitpa’el and “whose agent is not named” were cut, the “First:” places having grown with each parasha); they now open page viii under “CONJUGATIONS, CONTINUED”. A front-matter build that is sent has no such warning.
- **A parasha inside the book** opens with a title page of its own, like Bereshit's half-title: פָּרָשַׁת נֹחַ, a short rule, Parashat Noach (David, row E1, Oct 2026). Set `title_page: true` and `title_en` in parasha.json; render.py builds the page and layout.js then leaves out the on-page heading. A parasha that starts mid-chapter shows the chapter with its first verse (6:9 / [ו] ט, row E2).
- **One book, page numbers carried on.** (David's plan, Oct 2026.) The whole Chumash is one book, built a parasha at a time. Each new parasha starts on the page after the last page of the one before; never restart at 1. Always take the current page numbers, status and next_first_page from book.json (never from a skill or a note). The full book (front matter i–x + final parashot) is 133 pages. When a page count changes, rebuild every later parasha and its Key Insights booklet (the booklet cites page numbers). The page count of every parasha is kept in book.json in the GitHub repo (see commentary-book-build). The front matter (title, preface, key to terms, note on the vowels) appears once, at the start of the book. Later the book may be split by size into volumes, e.g. as the Seforno edition does (Bereshit + Shemot in one, Vayikra, Bamidbar and Devarim in the other), or into five, one per sefer; David decides that later, so keep each sefer's pages easy to split off.
- Licensing and print set-up are not decided yet (rows C1 Keep "decide at a later date", C2 unanswered): don't change credits, trim size, margins or font embedding until David decides.
- **One Chromium version per parasha** (29 Oct 2026, from David's "Pinning the Chromium/Playwright version ... would keep future rebuilds identical"). Page breaks depend on the browser: Noach, laid out with Chromium 141, rebuilds at 38 pages instead of 39 with Chromium 153. make_pdf.py records the version in build/rep.json; book.json keeps each parasha's version ("chromium"); check_all's pages check fails when they differ. A new parasha takes the version of its draft 1 and keeps it to the final. Bereshit, Noach and Lech Lecha: 141 (Playwright 1.56); Vayera: 153 (Playwright 1.63). Rebuild an older parasha only where its version is installed, or ask David before re-paginating.
- **One shared set of build scripts** (David, 9 Nov 2026, on the list of improvements: “Do all of them”): Noach on builds with shared/ (scripts, fonts, hyphenation); each parasha's scripts/*.py is a stub that runs the shared script on its folder. Proof before the switch: every parasha built both ways, the same HTML (font path aside), the same layout reports, all 152 pages the same picture for picture (shared/README.md). Bereshit keeps its own (it opens the volume). A change to shared/ changes every parasha's next build: only through an answered row, and each parasha it touches rebuilt with its Chromium version.
