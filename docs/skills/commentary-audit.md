# Audit a written parasha against the current rules

Use when David asks to check, audit or bring up to date work already done ("go through everything we have done and make sure it is consistent", "audit Noach"), and for each round of the whole-book audit begun 30 Oct 2026 (Bereshit to Vayera). Load **commentary-house-rules** first. Repo: davidross01/ibn-ezra-commentary at /home/claude/ibn-ezra-commentary. The checklist is `review_audit/checklist.md`; the audit's current state is in `docs/HANDOFF.md` ("Whole-book audit"). The repo copy of this skill is `docs/skills/commentary-audit.md`.

## David's settings (30 Oct 2026)
- **Pure rule fixes are applied directly, with a log**, and listed at the end of the review doc: grammar terms by the glossary, vowel signs, Hashem / ה׳, place names, Text lines that report no difference, hedges ("probably", "may", "seems"), markup, Ibn Ezra quoting a verse in that verse's own English, a misprint quoted in its corrected form, leftover placeholders.
- **Judgment calls are review rows**: translation, wording, notes. Brackets are **Ibn Ezra-led**: wherever he explains a word of the verse, the verse shows his reading in [square brackets].
- **Ibn Ezra's ideas** (his rule of 30 Oct): Part F, below.
- **Nothing is rebuilt during the audit.** At the end every parasha is rebuilt once (Chromium 153), book.json "chromium" becomes 153 for all, and the pages are renumbered once.
- **Order**: calibration on Bereshit 1 (Audit 1, applied 30 Oct), then **one doc per parasha, in great detail — "Don't escape anything"** (David, 30 Oct); Chayei Sarah waits.
- **The policy from Audit 1** (confirmed 30 Oct; docs/rules/translation.md "How far the audit goes", notes.md "Idea notes: length and sources"): brackets only where Ibn Ezra explains how a word of *that* verse works — no identifications, no book-wide word swaps, no reading carried over from another verse; translation rows only for mistranslations and inconsistencies, not smoothing; how Hashem prints stays. A kind of row he answered Keep is not raised again; a kind he answered Approve is raised again as rows. **Bracket rows only for clear cases** (1 Nov 2026, after Audit 2): where the verse band contradicts his reading or hides it entirely.

## Steps
1. `git pull`; `python3 -I scripts/series_status.py`; read the checklist.
2. Dump each chapter: `python3 -I review_audit/dump.py <folder> <ch> > <scratchpad>/<x>.txt` and read **all** of it, to the last line (it has no final newline). Keep running findings in `review_audit/<part>_findings.md` as you read, so nothing is lost.
3. Sources: fetch what the round needs in the Composio workbench and keep it in `review_audit/sources/` (the shell cannot reach Sefaria); Avi Ezer on all of Genesis is in `src/avi_ezer_genesis.json`. Idea paragraphs follow the **ibn-ezra-idea-notes** skill.
4. Programs: `python3 -I scripts/check_all.py <folder>` (letters, vowels, misprints, Hebrew sources, partly pointed, Text lines, Onkelos, Ideas); `python3 -I scripts/ideas_scan.py <folder>`; compare every "(Genesis c:v)" quotation in the English with that verse's English in the book; count conventions across the four parashot (square vs round brackets in the verse band, glossary terms, Text-line remarks, pointing remarks, "A reader might think") to see where an older parasha differs.
5. Read verse by verse, with the checklist: the verse band against every comment on it and on the verses before and after; the English commentary against the Hebrew, sentence by sentence (mistranslations first); each note (grammar in three steps; the ideas paragraph; Onkelos, Rashi, Radak, Rashbam, Ramban by their rules; every name, language and reference identified; no hedges; Text lines only for differences). Check sources on Sefaria (Onkelos from `src/onkelos.json`; the Sefaria MCP for Rashi, Radak, Avi Ezer, Rambam and Ibn Ezra elsewhere). Never name Strickman, Silver, Weiser or Krinsky — Mechokekei Yehudah (Yahel Ohr, Karnei Ohr) is Krinsky's.
6. **Rule fixes**: a script `review_audit/<part>_fixes.py` that writes a GENERATED block — Bereshit: C entries in `bereshit/work/changes.py` (`'@DELETE_BLOCK'` removes a block), then `python3 -I bereshit/work/build_model.py`; other parashot: their `work/chN.py` or `review_round/subs.py`, then `work/assemble.py`. Every Hebrew string is copied from the model or a source file, never typed. Log `review_audit/<part>_fixes_log.json`; doubts and pointing remarks taken out of notes go to `review_grammar/all/editorial_record.json`. Apply the fixes in memory one after another, so each `old` is checked against the text the earlier ones leave, and skip a fix whose old equals its new. Text lines: copy the printed form unpointed and the correction pointed from the Hebrew commentary's (printed) [correction] pair, matched by consonants (model: `ber2_fixes.py` F4). Print every change as a diff and read it. check_all must stay clean apart from the pages check and Ideas.
7. **Rows**: `review_audit/<part>_rows.py` as data with markers — `{vw:c:v:i-j}` verse words, `{vh:c:v}`, `{hcw:c:v:i-j}` Ibn Ezra's Hebrew (`|strip`, `|first`), `{hcb:c:v:k}` the k-th lemma section of his Hebrew, `{ec:c:v:n}` / `{nt:c:v:n}` Hebrew spans of the commentary / notes, `{q:…}` Scripture, `{o:…}` Onkelos; a row may name its `folder` and chapter `c`. Each row has `ops` (sec, (c, v), old, new). `python3 -I review_audit/make_rows.py <part>` resolves the markers, checks that each old occurs once, adds the Sefaria links and writes `<part>_rows.json` and `<part>_doc_cfg.json`; the rows module defines ROWS, PARTS and CFG (title, subtitle, how, clean — the applied fixes, ideas without a row and why, the sources read). Check every resolved excerpt and span index by printing them. No typed Hebrew in the rows file (grep for Hebrew outside markers). One row per item. Parts:
   - **A** the verse against Ibn Ezra; **B** the translation of his words (mistranslations marked); **C** grammar notes — "A reader might think …" and why it matters; **D** names, references, Onkelos and the other commentators; **E** book-wide print or conventions;
   - **F** Ibn Ezra's ideas (every review list has this Part): one row per idea paragraph, by the **ibn-ezra-idea-notes** skill — full for real ideas, 3–4 sentences for background, Avi Ezer for every idea and hint, Rambam on the same idea, sources read on Sefaria and named; no Aristotle, no Mechokekei Yehudah, Ramban's objections only if David asks. Models: F1 of Audit 1 (Vayera 18:21, as applied) and Audit 2 Part F.
8. **The doc**: commit and push; in the Composio workbench exec `scripts/review_doc.py`, `B = Builder(cfg)` (cfg from `<part>_doc_cfg.json` with `cfg['rows']` from `<part>_rows.json`), then `phase_main`, `phase_explanations`, `phase_styles`, `phase_links`, `verify` in separate cells; compare the md5 of the rows on both sides; confirm the owner is davross846 (GOOGLEDRIVE_GET_FILE_METADATA, `fileId`). Add the doc to `review_docs.json` (status open) and save `review_audit/<part>_doc_id.txt`; push, then run check-review-doc (`read_answers`). A new Part for an open doc: `B.append_part(doc_id, part, rows)`.
9. **Report** with the doc's link: what turned up, most serious first; what was applied; the rows by Part.

## Applying the answers
- `read_answers(<id>)` → `<part>_answers.json`. Discuss rows: explain, ask with checkboxes, save the answer to chat_answers.json.
- The applying script is `python3 -I review_audit/apply_answers.py <part>` (one script for every part; a Change row stops it and is handled by hand): it reads the rows' ops and the answers (chat answers override the chip), makes David's Change notes on the row's own text, writes Bereshit's C entries (row id as `row`) in a GENERATED block and other parashot's subs in their work files, and logs `<part>_applied.json`. Book-wide rows (a word changed everywhere, how Hashem prints) need their own script over every parasha, the front matter and the booklets.
- Each verse whose idea paragraph is done (or judged not needed, with the reason) goes into `<folder>/review/ideas.json` ("in review" → "done" or "kept as it was"; apply_answers.py does it); `--accept` never hides an Ideas hit, so the list is the audit's to-do.
- A sentence that an Onkelos entry owns (bereshit/work/onkelos.py, A/S entries) is changed there, not in changes.py — check_all "Onkelos" fails otherwise.
- Set the registry status to applied. Archive the doc only when David says (docs/rules/review_doc.md).

## At the end of the audit
Rebuild every parasha once with Chromium 153, set book.json pages and "chromium", renumber; check_all clean for each; then finalize-parasha (front matter, parts export, booklets with the wording in insights/davar.py, full book, Drive). Update README History and docs/HANDOFF.md.
